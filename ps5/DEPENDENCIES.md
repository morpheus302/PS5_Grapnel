# What Grapnel's console build uses from outside this repository

The exact revisions the console's `eboot.bin` of 2026-11-23 was compiled and linked with, read from the clones on our PC
and from the build's own records (the link's command, the driver's provenance file, the SDK's `.ps5-sdk-revision`). They
are pinned here because the console side of the port rests on them, and because some of these projects have changed
place before: keep your own copies.

None of them is copied into this repository except where a row says so. Nothing here contains a game file, a key, or
anything of Sony's: the payload SDK and `libc.prx` are clean-room work, as their own notices say.

## On the console (named, not provided)

Each has its own authors and licence; this repository does not explain how to jailbreak a console.

- A jailbroken PS5 with **kstuff**; Grapnel was tried on firmware **12.60 only**.
- An **ELF loader listening on 127.0.0.1:9021** (elfldr): the title sends its storage broker (`hookshot-broker.elf`) to it.
- **[ShadowMountPlus](https://github.com/drakmor/ShadowMountPlus) 1.7beta3 or newer** (1.7beta11 tried): it makes `/data`
  visible to the title and registers it.
- A way to copy files to the console (an FTP server, or similar).
